Tips & resources

A short guide from prep to cleanup, plus a checklist you can tick off as you go.

Prepare the room

Move furniture to the center and cover it and the floor with drop cloths. Remove outlet covers and tape off trim, window frames and ceilings.

Fix and clean the walls

Fill holes with spackle, sand smooth, then wipe dust and grease away with a damp cloth. Paint sticks best to clean, dry walls.

Prime when you need to

Prime bare drywall, stains and big color changes. A tinted primer close to your new color helps dark shades cover in fewer coats.

Test a sample first

Stick a sample on two walls and check it morning, afternoon and evening. Light changes color more than most people expect.

Cut in, then roll

Brush a 2-inch edge along corners and trim, then roll the wall in a W pattern. Keep a wet edge so the coats blend with no lap marks.

Clean up and store

Peel tape while the paint is slightly tacky. Wash brushes in warm soapy water and seal leftover paint tightly for touch-ups.
